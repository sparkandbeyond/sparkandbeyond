About Us

WHERE IT STARTED

Spark & Beyond wasn't built in a boardroom. It was built in a neighborhood, by someone who lived there and saw what was missing.

"Real development. Not just game day."
The Idea Behind Everything
Who We Are

COMMUNITY FIRST. SPORTS ARE THE SPARK.

Spark & Beyond is a youth sports organization built inside communities — not shipped in from the outside.

We partner with local venues, hire consistent coaches who stick around, and build programs around what each neighborhood actually needs. Not just sports, but the kind of relationships that make a neighborhood feel like home.

How It Started

BUILT FROM SCRATCH. ONE NEIGHBORHOOD AT A TIME.

01
The Problem

A neighborhood full of kids with nowhere to play together.

Travisso, TX had great courts, great families — and no program to bring them together. Parents were driving across town. Kids barely knew each other. That didn't sit right.

02
The Build

Start with a WhatsApp group. Build from there.

No corporate playbook. No franchise fee. Just a simple idea: find local coaches, use the community courts that already exist, keep it affordable. Travisso Sports League launched with a handful of families and a group chat. It now has 130+ families and runs every week.

03
What's Next

TSL proved the model. Spark & Beyond takes it further.

What worked in Travisso works anywhere. S&B is the engine for taking that same approach — community-first, coach-consistent, no club prices — to every neighborhood ready for it.

⚡ Founded 2026

THE FOUNDER STORY

Spark & Beyond started in Travisso, TX — not with a business plan, but with a gap nobody was filling. A teenager saw that suburban kids had nowhere to play that actually felt like theirs. So he built it. Travisso Sports League launched with a handful of families and a WhatsApp group. It now has 130+ families, real coaches, and real games every week.

Spark & Beyond is what happens when you take that model and bring it to the next neighborhood. And the one after that. Founder-led, community-driven, and already proven.
